About

I am a PhD student at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), advised by Prof. Jinwoo Shin. Prior to this, I received B.S. in Mathematical Science and Computer Science at KAIST in 2023. My research interests lie in representation learning and generative models, with a focus on robot foundation models that generalize across diverse and complex tasks. Recently, I have been particularly focused on developing generalist robot policies. As part of this, I had the opportunity to co-lead the development of RLDX-1, a robot foundation model trained at scale. My current work focuses on representation learning for video model-based robot policies (i.e., World Action Models), including improving the representations learned by video diffusion models and leveraging human video data to learn representations that transfer from human behavior to robotic manipulation.
